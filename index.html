Is this email phishing?

Paste in an email's subject and body. The detector looks for phishing keywords, known bad domains, fake brand websites like paypa1.com, and risky links, then rates the risk.

Risk is based on the total number of red flags: 5 or more is high, 2 to 4 is medium, and 0 or 1 is low. Keyword matching catches common tricks, but a carefully written phishing email can still get through, so always check the sender and hover over links before clicking.

Built by Hadis Pazhand. Originally written in Java; this web version uses the same detection rules so you can try it in your browser.

This is a student learning project, not a professional security tool. It can miss phishing emails and can flag safe ones, so don't rely on it to decide whether an email is safe. Everything runs in your browser and nothing you paste is sent or saved, but avoid pasting personal or sensitive information.
